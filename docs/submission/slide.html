LotaraRecall response for nursery kitchens
“A food product has been recalled. Which of our kitchens received the affected batch, and has someone acted?”

Lotara turns an official recall notice into a targeted, evidence-backed response: it matches each kitchen’s deliveries against the exact barcode + lot criteria, sends an AI investigator to collect the label evidence that is missing, and tracks a confirmed staff report until a manager closes the case.

01Who, problem, why now

For

The operations or food-safety lead at a caterer serving several nursery kitchens (crèches).

Problem

  • Recalls name batches, not brands. Name-only alerts flag every kitchen with the product.
  • Lot codes are often not recorded at delivery, so the answer stays “unknown”.
  • Follow-up across sites is phone calls and memory, with no evidence trail.

Why now

  • French recalls are open, machine-readable data (RappelConso).
  • Models can now read packaging labels and use tools, if exact rules stay in charge.

02How it works and where the data lives

1

Real RappelConso notice 2026-04-0331 saved with publication and retrieval times; deliveries per kitchen in Aiven PostgreSQL.

2

Exact rules sort every delivery: RECORDED MATCH NOT MATCHING NEEDS EVIDENCE

3

AI investigator (Claude, 7 allowlisted case-scoped tools, max 8 calls) finds the missing lot and asks that kitchen for a label photo.

4

AI reads the photo as unconfirmed and flags look-alikes (O vs 0). A person confirms; the rules re-check.

5

Kitchen sends a typed stock-check report; only the manager can mark REVIEWED COMPLETE.

Stored in Aivennotices, deliveries, cases, label photos, AI runs and tool steps, staff reports, event log
Guardrailsrules decide status; AI cannot override, cite unknown evidence or call food “safe”
